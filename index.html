03Using AI in this course

Follow the assistance mode specified for each activity. Explain your work and verify important behaviour independently.

Independent

No generative AI for the submitted attempt. Core concept checks and the final examination require independent work.

Guided

After your own attempt, use AI for explanation or critique within the stated task boundary. Record the assistance and what you checked.

Bounded development

The project permits AI generation and agent actions for specified implementation tasks, subject to review, independent verification and disclosure.

Record the tool, date, task, accepted and rejected changes, and verification evidence. Keep access keys and private or unapproved institutional data out of prompts and repositories. Read the full AI-use and academic-integrity policy.
